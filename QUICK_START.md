## 📱 Connect Telegram

1. Ensure your `TELEGRAM_BOT_TOKEN` is set in your `.env` file.
2. Open Telegram and search for your bot by its username (e.g., @YourBotName).
3. Send **`/start`** to your bot. This will register your chat ID as the admin.
4. Send **`/initall`**

**Done!** You now have God Mode control.
